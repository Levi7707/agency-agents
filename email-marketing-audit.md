### The Subscription Opt-Out Crisis

The most damaging finding: the majority of your paying customers are **not subscribed to marketing**. From the customer data sample:

- Customers like Patrick Rundquist (€50), Larissa Richter (€77.98), Bart Bakhuizen (€171.96), Stephen Scott (€76.40), Susan Blake (€95.69), and 40+ others are **confirmed buyers with zero marketing consent**.
- These people already trusted you enough to spend €50–€172. They are your warmest audience — and you cannot reach them legally by email.

**Why this happened:** Shopify's checkout default during 2024 had the marketing opt-in checkbox unchecked by default. Most buyers clicked through without opting in.

**What you can do:**
1. Enable Shopify's post-purchase "Thank you" page with a prominent marketing opt-in prompt
2. In your next transactional email (order confirmation), add a soft opt-in CTA: "Want tips on acing your airline assessment? [Yes, keep me updated]"
3. For buyers who haven't opted in: a one-time re-permission campaign is legally allowed under GDPR via legitimate interest — but must be carefully structured. Consult a GDPR-compliant email lawyer before contacting opted-out EU customers.

~30–40% of your subscribed list has never purchased. This includes:

These contacts are **warm leads sitting untouched**. A 3-email nurture sequence could convert 5–15% of them.

AVADA is installed on the store. It is doing nothing. Every day this stays unconfigured is revenue left on the table.

## Part 8 — Quick Wins: This Week in AVADA

### 1. Turn On Abandoned Cart Recovery Today (2 hours)
In AVADA → Automations → Abandoned Cart → Enable the default 3-email sequence. Customize subject lines using the swipe file above. This one action alone can recover €1,000+/month immediately.

### 2. Activate the Post-Purchase Welcome Email (1 hour)
In AVADA → Automations → Post-Purchase → Enable Email 1. Change the default copy to match the Email 1 from Automation 1 above. Even a single post-purchase email is infinitely better than silence.

### 3. Build Your 6 Segments (2 hours)
In AVADA → Contacts → Segments → Create the 6 segments defined in Part 3. This unlocks targeted campaigns immediately and prevents sending wrong offers to wrong people (eBook buyers don't need to be pitched on eBooks).

### 4. Send the Mock Interview Broadcast This Week
Copy the email from Part 4 word for word. Send to all buyers (minus the 1 person who already purchased the Mock Interview). Schedule for Tuesday or Wednesday 9:00 AM CET. This is the single highest-upside action in the entire audit — 2,230 buyers, never pitched, dormant coaching revenue.

### 5. Fix the Checkout Opt-In Rate
In Shopify Admin → Settings → Checkout → Marketing consent → Change from "hidden" or "pre-selected off" to "pre-selected on" (check GDPR compliance for your EU customer base — France allows pre-selected opt-in at checkout with clear disclosure). This ensures every new buyer is added to your marketing list going forward. Your current list of opted-out buyers cannot be recovered without a re-permission campaign.
