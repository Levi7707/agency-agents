## Part 1 — The 5 Phases of the Store

### Phase 1 — Embryo (Jan – May 2024)
**142 orders · €4,125 revenue · AOV ~€29**

| Month | Orders | Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 2024 | 1 | €29 |
| Feb 2024 | 111 | €3,219 |
| Mar 2024 | 23 | €674 |
| Apr 2024 | 3 | €87 |
| May 2024 | 4 | €117 |

The store launched with a standalone eBook at **€28.99**. February was anomalously large (111 orders, likely a launch push or viral moment), then the store almost completely died — 23, 3, 4 orders in the following months.

At €29 per order, there was no room for paid acquisition. Revenue per order left almost no margin. This phase validated demand but confirmed the model was unscalable.

**The store was on life support by May 2024.**

---

### Phase 2 — Rebirth & Breakout (Jun – Jul 2024)
**264 orders · €17,051 · AOV ~€64**

| Month | Orders | Revenue | Conv. Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jun 2024 | 45 | €2,905 | 1.53% |
| Jul 2024 | 219 | €14,146 | 2.08% |

Something fundamental changed in June 2024: the product was overhauled into a multi-variant physical + eBook model at **€48.99–€84.99**, doubling the AOV from €29 to €64. This single pricing decision is responsible for the entire business.

July 2024 is the single best month in store history: 219 orders, €14,146, 2.08% conversion rate — with clean, high-intent European traffic.

---

### Phase 3 — Golden Era (Aug – Oct 2024)
**529 orders · €32,651 · Peak CVR 2.48%**

| Month | Orders | Revenue | Sessions | Conv. Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 2024 | 209 | €13,326 | 6,572 | **2.34%** |
| Sep 2024 | 173 | €11,014 | 5,757 | **2.48%** |

The store at its absolute best. **September 2024's 2.48% conversion rate is the peak of the entire store's history** — organic, targeted, high-intent European traffic converting cleanly. The product/market fit was working.

The gradual decline from 209 → 173 → 147 orders reflects natural audience saturation as the initial wave of the pilot community was reached.

---

### Phase 4 — Traffic Surge, Quality Crash (Nov – Dec 2024)
**292 orders · €17,322 · CVR collapsed to ~1%**

| Month | Orders | Revenue | Sessions | Conv. Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 2024 | 136 | €8,065 | **11,193** | 0.94% |
| Dec 2024 | 156 | €9,257 | **11,892** | 1.02% |

Sessions nearly doubled vs. October (5,769 → 11,193) but orders did not follow. This is the clearest signal that a paid campaign was launched with poor audience targeting. The store went from earning €1 per ~2.3 sessions to €1 per ~4.5 sessions — efficiency halved overnight.

Despite this, December 2024 at €9,257 is still the 3rd best month ever. The business remained healthy — but the seeds of the 2025 decline were planted here.

## Part 9 — Growth Hacker Verdict

### What built this business

1. **The June 2024 pricing pivot** — raising AOV from €29 to €64 by introducing physical format and variants is the single decision responsible for €130K+ in revenue. Without it, the business would have died in Q1 2024.

2. **Organic community distribution** — 51% dark social referrals mean the product is genuinely recommended between pilots. This trust-based word-of-mouth is rare and valuable.

3. **Product quality** — 0.42% returns and consistent 2%+ CVR at peak are objective proof that product-market fit is real and strong.

### What damaged it

1. **The November 2024 paid campaign** — doubled traffic, halved conversion rate, and the store never fully recovered. Low-quality traffic diluted the funnel and broke the algorithm's audience learning. This single campaign effectively cut monthly revenue by 50–65% going forward.

2. **Zero email strategy** — 2 orders from email in 2 years with 2,375+ buyer addresses is a critical missed channel. A basic 5-email post-purchase sequence alone could have added €15–25K in the period.

3. **The coaching offer was never launched properly** — Pilot Mock Interview at €399.99 had 1 sale in 2 years. At 1% conversion of book buyers, that represents ~€89,000 in unrealized revenue.

4. **No product diversification** — 97% single-product means every external shock (algorithm change, ad quality dip, seasonal variation) hits with no cushion.

### The 3 highest-leverage actions to recover peak performance

**1. Fix traffic quality first (protect CVR)**
Your September 2024 2.48% CVR proves the product converts brilliantly when the right people see it. One well-targeted €10/day Meta or Google campaign to European aviation communities (pilot Facebook groups, aviation forums, airline-specific audiences) beats €100/day broad targeting every time. Geo-target: FR, UK, DE, IT, ES, PL, PT, NL, BE, IE, GR first.

**2. Build the email revenue engine**
You have 2,000+ buyer email addresses sitting idle. A 5-email post-purchase sequence structured as follows could realistically add €3–5K/month with zero incremental ad spend:
- Day 2: Delivery confirmation + how to get the most from the book
- Day 7: Ask for a review (social proof compound)
- Day 14: Introduce the Mock Interview service (€399.99)
- Day 30: CV Correction offer (€79.99)
- Day 90: Re-engagement — "Your next airline application?"

**3. Activate the coaching funnel**
Create a dedicated landing page for the Mock Interview service, add 3 real testimonials, and make it the primary email offer to book buyers. At €399.99, you need just 5 sales per month to add €2K MRR. With 100+ new book buyers every month as a captive audience, this is the fastest path to doubling revenue.
